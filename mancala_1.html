▸ How to play

Rules of Mancala

  • Board: 6 pockets per side + 1 store (Mancala) each.
  • Start: 4 stones in each pocket.
  • Move: Pick any of your pockets (bottom row). Stones are distributed counter-clockwise, one per pocket — including your own store but not the opponent's store.
  • Extra turn: If the last stone lands in your store, you go again.
  • Capture: If the last stone lands in an empty pocket on your side, you capture that stone plus all stones in the opposite pocket — all go to your store.
  • End: When one side is empty, the other player collects all remaining stones. Most stones in store wins.
